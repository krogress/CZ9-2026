A module of the Long March 9

The development of the Long March 9 launch vehicle targets requirements such as crewed lunar exploration, Mars sample return, and construction of large-scale space facilities. Designed to carry out future missions including crewed lunar exploration and deep-space exploration, the Long March 9 is currently China's most powerful rocket under development. Its comprehensive performance indicators reach advanced international standards, enabling it to meet the demands of major national scientific and technological initiatives in deep-space exploration and crewed lunar missions for an extended period ahead. This will ensure that China ranks among the world's leading nations in launch vehicle technology by 2030.
